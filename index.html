My Dearest Dharan,

I don't think there will ever be enough words to perfectly explain how much you mean to me, but today I want to try anyway.

Since 1 April 2025, you've slowly become such a special part of my life. We've laughed, argued, annoyed each other, made memories, and somehow turned ordinary days into little pieces of our story.

One thing I'll always remember is how you looked after me whenever I was sick. Especially when I was hospitalized and you were trying to balance your internship with everything else.

You still made time for me. You still stayed. And during a time when I really needed someone, you were there.

That's something I'll never take for granted.

Then there are all the silly little things I love about us — like you letting me win arguments, and that unforgettable Terraform Tower moment where you apparently discovered the art of floating out of a seat. 😂

I hope you know how proud I am of you. I hope this year brings you closer to everything you dream of. I hope you become everything you want to become.

And selfishly, I hope I get to be beside you through as much of it as possible.

More birthdays. More adventures. More arguments that I definitely win. More ridiculous memories. More ordinary days that somehow become special just because they're with you.

Happy birthday, my love.
Thank you for being you.
And thank you for being mine.

Forever yours,
